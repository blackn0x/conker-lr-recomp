Rules copied from the game: wrap 0x000DFD90 (count code words, break at the last space or {PMARKER} once the line passes the wrap width; a longer word stays whole), size 0x000E0B5E (width + 50, height + 40, or + 60 at the line limit; over the limit: width + 60, at least 150, fixed height, the text scrolls), a..z drawn as small capitals (0.81), text scale 0.81 × 0.9, letter spacing 1.625 and line pitch 25 px measured on a game frame. Button icons are drawn as boxes (estimate 24 font px). The real bubble shape, tail and position differ; the size is what matters.
